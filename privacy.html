trueframe ♥

Privacy

Trueframe collects nothing.

Your photos go to your library and nowhere else.

No internet

Trueframe never connects to a network. There's no code for it.

No tracking

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

Camera

The camera is ready while Trueframe is open, and iOS shows its green dot. Photos are only taken while the phone is pressed to you and its proximity sensor is covered.

Your photos

During a session, photos are kept in the app's own temporary storage on your iPhone. When it ends, Trueframe chooses the best ones on your iPhone, adds them to your library and deletes the rest. Nothing is uploaded.

Photo library

Trueframe only asks to add photos. It can't see, read or change anything already in your library.

On your device

Your lens, flash and interval choices. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions

Camera, to take photos, and adding to Photos, to save them. You can revoke both anytime in Settings.

This applies to Trueframe for iPhone, made by sryo. Since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children. If this ever changes, this page changes first and the release notes will say so. The source is public, so you can check every line of this yourself.
