# trueframe ♥

Hold your phone to your heart to capture life.

No viewfinder, no shutter button. Press the phone to your chest and the
proximity sensor starts a burst of full-resolution photos. A heartbeat
haptic marks each shot. Lift the phone to end the session, watch the
moments tumble across the screen, and the best ones save themselves to
your photo library.

## Build

Requires Xcode 26 and an iPhone running iOS 26.

```
xcodegen generate
open Trueframe.xcodeproj
```
